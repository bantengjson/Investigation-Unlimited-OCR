<BLOCK>title<LEVEL1>[115, 653, 267, 667]<FORK>5.2. Main Results
<BLOCK>text[113, 672, 885, 899]<FORK>As visually summarized in Figure 6 and further detailed in Table 1 and Table 2, Unlimited OCR 2 takes a significant step toward the OCR 3.0 era, advancing document parsing along three key dimensions. First, it establishes the practical feasibility of hundred-page end-to-end parsing, achieving a score of 77.06 on MarathonDocBench and demonstrating its ability to handle documents at a scale beyond conventional multi-page settings. Second, it maintains robust parsing performance as document length increases. Through end-to-end modeling with SR-SWA, Unlimited OCR 2 filters redundant visual tokens while preserving relevant cross-page information, achieving the highest Overall scores and state-of-the-art performance on both cross-page text and table metrics, T-Text and T-Table, respectively, from dozens of pages on MPDocBench to hundreds of pages on MarathonDocBench. Third, it establishes a new state-of-the-art in long-document parsing, outperforming leading closed-source models and top-tier open-source MLLMs across these benchmarks. Furthermore, Unlimited OCR 2 retains strong single-page recognition capabilities, achieving an Overall score of 94.02% on OmniDocBench in Table 3, confirming that its sparse attention design preserves fine-grained local recognition.
